# Asset provenance: what in this repository is ours

Written on 2026-09-24 because the owner asked the question directly: *公开仓库里除了那个 wwise 的文件，其余的包括美术
资源等"空庭"自有自用的部分，是否都是我们自作的？如果不是，就要进行自作自建* -- with the standing note that S.M.Y.T. is a
non-governmental, non-profit organisation, so a credit line is enough and watermarks are not wanted.

Every asset that ships was classified by opening it and reading what it says about itself, not by assuming. The
result is one item that is **not** ours and has to be redrawn, and three groups that are third-party, standard,
and either required or deliberately excluded.

## What follows from this

1. **The letterforms are to be redrawn.** Two attempts on 2026-09-24 (`art/textura_glyphs.py`) produced something
   geometric rather than blackletter and were not shipped. A convincing textura is type design rather than
   geometry, and the work continues; until it lands, the OFL face is used **with its licence and its name kept**,
   which is a permitted arrangement and not a hidden one.
2. **`audio/wwise/` has left the public mirror** (owner's instruction, 2026-09-24: *先把 wwise 的部分移出公开仓库*).
   Most of it is the author's own project data, but the `Factory*` work units and the generated `.bnk` are
   Audiokinetic's, and the generated bank is a build artefact besides. The boundary is the whole directory rather
   than a list of files inside it, and the directory stays in the working repository, where the project lives.
3. **Flutter's templates stay and are credited.** Removing them would mean hand-writing the platform hosts, which
   would be worse software and no more ours. They carry BSD-3, and the `NOTICE` file is where that is recorded.
4. **No watermarks.** Per the owner: S.M.Y.T. is a non-governmental, non-profit organisation, so attribution in
   files is enough and nothing is stamped onto the artwork.
